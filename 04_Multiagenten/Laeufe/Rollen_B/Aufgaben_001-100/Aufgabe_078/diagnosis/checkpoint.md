- Root UI path: `useVoiceBroadcastPlayback.ts` returns `live` from info state alone (line 60); `VoiceBroadcastHeader.tsx` accepts a boolean and renders `LiveBadge` for every truthy value (lines 30, 57). `LiveBadge.tsx` has no grey prop and `_LiveBadge.pcss` fixes its background to `$alert`. Thus stopped local playback, buffering, and playing can share the same red badge while info remains started/resumed.
- Playback model: `VoiceBroadcastPlayback.ts` has no liveness field/getter/event. `setState` and `setInfoState` emit their own changed events but do not recompute combined liveness. `start` enters Buffering without a chunk; `playEvent` enters Playing; `playNext` enters Buffering at the tail of a live broadcast or Stopped at the tail of a stopped one; `stop`, `pause`, and `resume` change state. These are the transition points to cover. `setDuration` and `setPosition` already suppress duplicate events.
- `VoiceBroadcastChunkEvents.ts` sorts by sequence when present or timestamp and has `getNext`, but no `isLast`. Last-event detection should check membership and sorted tail, especially after replacement by transaction ID.
- Header call sites: playback body, recording body, recording pip, and pre-recording pip. The two recording bodies pass booleans; pre-recording omits `live` and needs an explicit `not-live` if the prop becomes required.
- Existing model tests cover buffering before first chunk, playing on arrival, buffering after current final chunk, and stopping after the final chunk of a stopped broadcast; they do not assert badge grey styling or liveness events. Header tests pass booleans and snapshots reflect the old contract.
- Uncertainty: issue text explicitly assigns grey to buffering, live to playing, and not-live to stopped/info stopped, but does not give a precise precedence for paused playback versus paused info. The general description calls paused grey, so treating either pause as grey when the broadcast has not stopped is the plausible interpretation. Archived broadcasts can play chunks while info is stopped; the stated mapping says their liveness remains not-live.
