The lifecycle gap is in [poll.go](/app/internal/storage/fs/poll.go:12): `Poll` receives its context and update callback at launch, but `Poller` retains neither a cancellation function nor a way to wait for the goroutine. It also does not stop its ticker.

All five backends launch a poller without retaining it: [Git](/app/internal/storage/fs/git/store.go:126), [local](/app/internal/storage/fs/local/store.go:43), [S3](/app/internal/storage/fs/object/s3/store.go:79), [Azure Blob](/app/internal/storage/fs/object/azblob/store.go:69), and [OCI](/app/internal/storage/fs/oci/store.go:56). Their `SnapshotStore` types have no `Close()` method. The likely fix is to give `Poller` a stored `UpdateFunc`, internal cancellable context, and completion signal; each backend should retain its poller and have `Close()` cancel and wait for it. Git’s fixed-hash path starts no poller, so `Close()` must be safe there too.

Existing tests use context cancellation during cleanup but do not establish that `Close()` waits for an active update. A further uncertainty is that the Git, local, S3, and Azure update methods currently ignore their context, so shutdown may have to wait for an in-flight update to finish.
